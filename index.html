TallyCraft

Your workday, already written down.

TallyCraft is an iPhone app for solo mobile-trade operators — landscapers, cleaners, detailers, and the like. It quietly notices when you stop to work during the day, lets you confirm those stops each evening with one tap, and keeps the record of your jobs, what they're worth, and who hasn't paid yet.

Support

Your data

TallyCraft collects as little as possible and never sells or shares your data for advertising. The full, plain-English details — what the app collects, why, where it lives, and the controls you have — are in the privacy policy. You can export your recent records and delete your account, and everything it holds, from inside the app at any time.
